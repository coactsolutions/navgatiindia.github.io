// The Equipment

A calibrated hit. A perfect readout.

A steel plate drops. The road answers back. In milliseconds, our FWD-equipped vehicle reads exactly how your pavement responds to a load that mimics real traffic — no coring, no cutting, no lane closures for days. Just a true picture of structural strength, layer by layer, at highway speed.

  • Non-destructive — the road stays intact, the data doesn't lie
  • Every drop simulates a real moving wheel load
  • Deflection captured at every single test point on the route
  • Built for continuous, large-scale network surveys — not one-off spot checks
